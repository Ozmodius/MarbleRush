PlaneTlt

Tilt the board. Roll to the rescue.

Baron Von Ratchet has caged Rolle's friends deep in the mazes of five planets. Tilt through fifty mazes, explore them for the fuel cells that fly Rolle's ship from planet to planet, and free every friend. Free to play on your phone, tablet or computer.

The mission

Three jobs on every planet.

Every maze asks the same of you, and the ready screen before each one lists what is left: escape it, then go back in and explore it for its fuel cell. On each tenth floor, free the friend the Baron has caged there.

1Escape the maze

Tilt the board and roll the marble to the glowing exit, past every hole and trap. Fall in and you start again.

  • 50 mazes
  • Bronze, silver & gold times

2Explore for the fuel cell

Beaten a maze? Shrink down and explore it from the inside. Each one hides a fuel cell in its deepest dead end, and only Explore mode shows it. Rolle's ship runs on them: no fuel, no next planet.

  • 3 from Sawturn fly you to Slipstonia
  • 5 to Magmars
  • 7 for each planet after

3Free your friend

Floor 10 of each planet holds one of Rolle's friends in the Baron's cage. Roll through it to set them free. The exit stays locked until you do.

  • 5 friends to free
  • Each joins your crew

The story

Rolle to the rescue.

Baron Von Ratchet wound up five planets like clocks. To keep them ticking, he kidnapped Rolle's friends from MarbleTopia and set them rolling his maze-engines.

  • Rolle's ship drops you in. Explore each planet's mazes for its fuel and the ship flies you on to the next, closer to the Baron.
  • Every friend you free joins your crew, with a trick of their own.
  • The Baron left his diary lying around. Find its pages to learn what he is really up to.

The planets

Five planets. Fifty mazes. Fifteen ways to fall.

Every maze floats high above its planet. Each planet throws three new traps at you, one at a time, then all at once. Explore its mazes for enough fuel cells and Rolle's ship flies on to the next.

Sawturn

A wooden workbench that sprouts into a forest as you go. Pip is caged on floor 10.

  • Holes
  • Moving gates
  • Conveyor belts

Slipstonia

Snowy peaks and slick blue ice. Good luck stopping. Flurry waits on floor 10.

  • Ice
  • Wind fans
  • Falling icicles

Magmars

Black rock over rivers of lava. Don't look down. Cinder is held on floor 10.

  • Lava flares
  • Molten gates
  • Geysers

Bouncelot

Plastic bricks, bouncy bumpers and springs that fling you. Bobble is trapped on floor 10.

  • Bumpers
  • Spring pads
  • Spinning arms

Gearth

The Baron's own foundry: magnets, crushers and live rails. Rivet is locked away on floor 10.

  • Magnets
  • Crushers
  • Electric rails

How it plays

Tilt your phone. Feel the marble roll.

Lean your phone and the marble rolls, slides and bounces like it's really in your hands. On a computer, use the arrow keys or drag with the mouse.

  • Pick a landing site on the planet and drop in. Most mazes take under a minute. Just one more go...
  • Close calls. Skim a hole, slip past a gate, make it by a hair.
  • Race the medal clock. It runs on screen against the gold time, then silver, then bronze.
  • Coins on risky paths. Grab them for the store, if you dare the detour.

The crew

Every marble is a character.

Nine marbles, each with a handling all their own and a signature trick they can pull once a run. Cinder shrugs off a lava flare. Glint sees through the Baron's false walls. Bumper hops right back out of a hole.

  • Grow their bond by clearing mazes with them.
  • Upgrade each one along their own track, all the way to a glowing aura.
  • Skins and trails, from a tiny Earth to a blazing comet of flame.

Secrets

Every maze is worth exploring.

The view from above never shows the whole story. Go inside a maze in Explore mode and it gives up what it hides.

  • Walls that aren't walls hide the ten pages of the Baron's diary, there only when you explore.
  • Glint's Clear Sight, tapped while exploring, makes false walls shimmer and lights the fuel cell and any diary page.
  • Survey a maze by rolling over nine tenths of its floor.

Explore mode

Now jump inside the maze.

Beaten a level from above? Shrink down and roll through it from the inside. The walls tower over you, the traps loom up close, and you have to find your own way out.

  • The only way to find fuel cells. One hides in every maze, and the ship won't fly on without them.
  • Steer it yourself with an on-screen stick, or keys and mouse.
  • Behind the marble, or through its eyes. Your pick.
  • Explorer kit: a compass to the exit, and a map that draws itself as you go.

Every day

Something new every time you come back.

A brand-new daily maze, a seven-day reward calendar, three missions a day and 27 achievements to chase.

  • Player levels that unlock skins, trails and power-ups as you climb.
  • A daily streak that builds to a big day-seven prize.
  • Chase gold: every maze has bronze, silver and gold times, and a leaderboard to top.
